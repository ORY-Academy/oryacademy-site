Privacy notice

ORY Academy CIC organises meetings and courses for clinicians. This notice explains what personal information we collect through this website and our sign-up and registration forms, and how we use it.

Who we are

ORY Academy CIC is a community interest company registered in England and Wales, company number 17488094. Our registered office is Suite 10, Capital House, 61 Amhurst Road, London E8 1LL. We decide how the information described here is used. You can contact us at info@oryacademy.com.

What this website collects

This website does not ask you to create an account and does not collect personal information itself. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics in future, they will load only after you choose Accept.

Forms and emails

Mailing list. If you join our mailing list we collect your name and email address, and your consent. We use them to tell you about future ORY Academy meetings and courses, and we send a confirmation by email. Every email contains an unsubscribe link, and you can leave at any time.

Registration, feedback and certificates. If you register for a meeting or course, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, role, workplace, email address, dietary requirements and the details needed to take payment (we do not collect card numbers on this website). We use them to run the meeting, to take payment, to keep a record of attendance, to ask for your feedback and to issue certificates. The feedback form asks whether we may share your email address with the meeting’s sponsors, and we share it only if you say yes. Sponsors receive the names, roles and workplaces of people who attended, but not their contact details unless you agreed.

We rely on your consent for the mailing list, and on our legitimate interest in running the meetings you register for, and on the need to take payment, for registrations.

Services this website relies on

Our forms are provided by Tally, which stores what you submit on our behalf. Our emails are sent using Resend. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts are supplied by Google Fonts. Programmes and calendar files may open from Google Drive. These services may record technical information, such as your IP address, when their content loads or when you follow a link to them.

Cookies

This website sets only the cookies and similar storage it needs to work, plus a note of your cookie choice on your own device. Declining does not affect how you use the site. Forms and other content supplied by Tally, Google and others may set their own cookies when they load. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings in the footer.

How long we keep information

We keep mailing list details until you unsubscribe. We keep registration and attendance records for as long as we need them to run our meetings, to issue certificates and to meet our accounting duties.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to object to how we use it, by emailing info@oryacademy.com. If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
