About the meeting

The 2nd Medical Rhinology Meeting brings together consultants from immunology, haematology, dermatology, respiratory medicine, rheumatology and ENT surgery for a single day of practical, case-based teaching.

Organised by consultant ENT surgeons Ms Sevina Tzortzis and Mr Aman Khanna, the meeting looks at how our colleagues in other specialties see the patient with nasal and sinus disease, and how we can work together to treat them better.

What the meeting will cover

Immunology and immunotherapy

Immunological presentations in rhinology, and a panel on immunotherapy pathways.

Haematology in rhinology

Bleeding and haematological conditions that present to the rhinology clinic.

Dermatology and laser

Skin conditions affecting the nose, and the role of laser in rhinology.

Facial pain

A practical approach to facial pain in rhinology.

Respiratory disease and biologics

The unified airway, and a panel on biologics in sinonasal disease.

Rheumatology

Rheumatic conditions with nasal and sinus manifestations.

Service development

Setting up medical rhinology services, digital health and PROMs, and building a Midlands network.
